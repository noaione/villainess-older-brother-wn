# Epilogue: Desire

Even after returning from the dungeon, I had no time to rest. Naturally—on the
surface, a stampede had just occurred. There were countless post-incident tasks
to handle, and as a noble, I had to deal with a mountain of responsibilities. To
make matters worse, the mastermind was Earl Roeme, the domain’s lord, along with
his close aides, which only added to the workload. Until the royal capital’s
dispatched officials arrived, I was swamped.

From the documents I reviewed, the stampede’s damage was kept to a minimum. With
Caron and the others running rampant, this outcome was expected.

Even Dijott, which I’d been worried about, was defended with minimal damage
thanks to Princess Arianorth’s leadership and the efforts of Yudai and other key
fighters. Well, with three light mages present, the combatants likely fought
without hesitation.

The only significant casualties were the domain’s soldiers stationed near the
dungeon. Even then, my subordinates retrieved them, and Caron healed them.
They’re now back to their duties, hale and hearty.

For a stampede involving thirty thousand magical beasts, this is a miraculous
result.

Moreover, during this battle, the protagonist heroes and the Saintess gained new
powers. In Dijott specifically, Princess Arianorth and the others greatly
boosted their reputations. The story progressed as in the original, but with far
less damage—an excellent outcome.

All of this is thanks to everyone’s efforts. This time, I barely contributed, so
I can’t thank them enough. I’ll need to reward everyone who fought so hard once
we return to the royal capital. I have a good guess about what they’ll ask for,
though.

By the way, the other students and Feibeln were sent back to the royal capital
earlier. There was no point in keeping them here, and they had no reason to stay
in Dijott. They’re probably back to their usual student lives by now.

But enough tangents.

After handing over duties to the royal capital’s dispatched officials, I
returned to the private room assigned to me.

There, I found an unexpected guest.

“Marina, what’s up?”

Today, we’re scheduled to return via my \[Phase Connection Gate\]. The others
should be packing, and that includes her.

“I wanted to talk for a bit.”

Marina’s expression is slightly stiff, and she’s fidgeting with the ends of her
hair, clearly nervous.

Though puzzled, I gesture for her to sit on the sofa and take a seat across from
her.

“…”

“…”

For a while, we sit in silence.

Sensing no sign of her speaking first, I decided to break the ice to help her
relax.

“How’s Maimu doing?”

Maimu is one half of the composite spirit Marina contracted. She’s quite timid
and spends most of her daily life with Marina, but she doesn’t seem to be here
now.

Marina stumbles over her words with an “Uh, well…”

“She’s with Noma-chan. You know, Maimu-chan and the others are like kids, right?
They lack some common sense as spirits, so Noma-chan’s teaching them the ropes.”

She flashes a bright smile, adding, “Noma-chan’s been a huge help\~\!”

Maimu’s still scared of me, but she’s built good relationships with the others.
She seems especially close with Caron, Minerva, Orca, and Shion, who have fire
or water affinities.

Noma, for her part, is thrilled to have her first spirit juniors and is going
all out. She’s even serving her prized magical cuisine, so it’s only a matter of
time before Maimu develops a refined palate.

“That’s good to hear. She seems to be fitting in with the Foranada crew, so
things should be fine back in the royal capital, too.”

“Yeah. She’s a bit shy, but I don’t think it’s a dealbreaker.”

“Got it.”

So, the issue is probably my presence. I’d be lying if I said it didn’t sting,
but there’s no helping it. Until she grows more comfortable, I’ll adjust by
limiting contact.

“How’s your training going?”

Since returning from the dungeon, Marina’s been seriously honing her spirit
magic. I’ve been too busy to check on her, but Noma’s been overseeing her
progress.

Marina nods briskly.

“It’s going great\! It’s like all the frustration from before was a lie—I can do
so many new things now\!”

Her beaming smile shows she’s genuinely thrilled with her progress. I knew she’d
been struggling with her stagnant growth, so her improvement warms my heart,
too.

“Heh, that’s great news.”

“\!”

When I soften my eyes and smile, Marina’s face flushes red, and she looks away.

Apparently, my smile made her shy. It’s an adorable reaction, but I can’t help
wondering if I’m _that_ charming. It’s a bit embarrassing.

She’s gotten better, but she still turns a bit clumsy around me, as always.

I keep my amusement to myself. Showing it would only make her clumsier, and we’d
never get to the point.

Waiting for her to calm down, I steer toward the main topic.

“So, what did you want to talk about?”

“It’s… well…”

She hesitates for a moment.

But after taking a deep breath, she continues.

“It’s about my status as your concubine. I wanted to discuss it properly.”

“…I see.”

I nod in understanding, though inwardly, I’m surprised. Honestly, I thought
Marina would let the current ambiguous relationship slide, gradually cementing
her position as a “de facto concubine.” I figured she’d avoid bringing it up
until the rumors solidified.

Unaware of my thoughts, Marina presses on.

“I got the chance to become stronger. And I _did_ become stronger. I think I’ve
honed my skills enough to fend off assassins on my own now.”

That’s true. Paired with Maimu, Marina’s level exceeds 90\. She’s also gained
extraordinary perception abilities. At this point, neither frontal assaults nor
assassinations would work against her.

In other words, there’s no longer a need for me to protect her.

“Do you want to leave my protection?”

I ask, a bit teasingly, though I don’t think that’s her intent.

She seems to catch the playful jab and shakes her head calmly.

“No\~ My wish hasn’t changed from the start. I want to stay by your side,
Prince… Zex-sama. I want to love you and be loved by you. It’s the dream I’ve
held since I was a child.”

Her words carry a powerful resolve. An unshakable conviction and unrelenting
determination shine through. She will never give up.

This is the essence of Marina, the heroine.

I’m filled with awe.

In the original story, she was the girl I admired most. Though reality differs
from the source, her core remains unchanged. No matter how weak she is, how
harsh her circumstances, or how much others refuse to acknowledge her, Marina
never wavers from her chosen desire. She struggles, fights, and persists. Her
beauty lies in walking that gritty yet dazzling path.

In the original, she settled for a resigned conclusion, “protecting Yudai’s
home.” But in reality, her struggles bore fruit. She gained wings in the form of
a spirit.

And so, Marina will keep soaring—toward the fulfillment of her desire.

Why me, though?

It’s a question I’ve asked myself countless times. Each time a wonderful woman
confesses her love, my heart aches. I cry out that I’m not worthy of such
affection.

But I don’t let it show. No matter how little confidence I have, these women
chose me. Denying that would be more than rude—it would be disrespectful.

If I lack confidence, I’ll forge myself until I’m worthy. To wallow in
self-pity, thinking “I’m not good enough,” is the cowardice of a tragic heroine
wannabe. I may be ordinary, but I won’t stoop that low.

So, I meet Marina’s gaze head-on. Her radiant eyes make me want to look away,
but I hold steady.

“I can’t return your feelings right now.”

“I… see…”

Disappointment flickers across her face. It hurts, but this is a line I can’t
cross. Until I overcome Caron’s fated death, I have no room to look elsewhere.

Yet, faced with such profound love, running away isn’t an option. It’s
unthinkable. I want to be worthy of this earnest woman.

I gently take Marina’s right hand and press my lips to its back.

“But I promise. By the time we graduate from the academy, I’ll give you my
answer.”

“…”

Marina’s eyes widen, and she freezes in shock.

Gradually, comprehension dawns. Her face flushes bright red, and her mouth opens
and closes with soft “ah, ah” sounds. Then, large tears begin to stream down her
cheeks.

“Huh\!?”

Her sudden sobbing catches even me off guard.

I understand it’s from joy, but a beautiful woman’s tears are startling
nonetheless.

While I flounder, Marina laughs through her tearful sobs.

“Thank you, Zex-sama. I’m… I’m so happy…”

I stay by her side, comforting her until her tears subside.

As a result, our return to the royal capital is slightly delayed, and Caron and
the others grill me relentlessly—a charming consequence.
