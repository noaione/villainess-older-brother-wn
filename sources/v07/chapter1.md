March. The harsh winter, with its piercingly clear air, had quieted down, and a
faint, warm sunlight began to show its face.

The signs of spring were especially noticeable in nature. In the forests of the
towering mountains in a remote corner of the Foranada earldom, flower buds
heralding the arrival of spring were sprouting in abundance.

Amidst this grand natural setting, three figures were present.

One was me, Zex. I’m the current head of the Foranada Earldom, and it wouldn’t
be an exaggeration to call myself the world’s strongest human. My naturally
white hair is a mark of being “colorless,” unable to use magic, but I’ve managed
to overcome that through research and effort.

I have a mission I must fulfill: to save my beloved younger sister, Caron—also
known as Caroline—from a fated death. This world is nearly identical to the
setting of a game I played in my previous life, and if things proceed as they
are, Caron will die.

Is there any brother who would silently stand by while his precious sister faces
death? —No, absolutely not\!

Thus, I’ve exhausted every possible means. Not only have I strengthened myself,
but I’ve also cherished my connections with those around me and moderately
empowered the servants of my domain. Naturally, I’ve made Caron and the others
stronger as well.

It’s not just martial prowess. I’ve spared no effort in developing economic and
productive capabilities for self-sufficiency, and I take pride in having
enriched the domain to a level that could be called world-class.

Thanks to these efforts, the environment surrounding Caron and me has changed
significantly. The number of important people in my life has grown, each with a
kind heart. I’ve been steadily crushing the death flags related to Caron.
Defeating the demon possessing her was, I believe, a particularly significant
achievement.

... I’ve digressed too much. Let’s get back on track.

As mentioned earlier, there were two others besides me in the forest.

One was Marina. Her deep aqua hair was tied into a single ponytail flowing over
her shoulder, and her countenance was strikingly beautiful. Though a commoner by
birth, her appearance was refined enough to be mistaken for a noblewoman. Her
figure exuded femininity, and her overall demeanor radiated a soothing, gentle
aura—a true “healing-type” woman.

In the original story, she was a heroine targeted by the hero’s party. Yet,
through some twist of fate, she ended up developing feelings for me.

It’s a complete deviation from the original plot, but I can’t deny Marina’s
feelings. Besides, I find her earnest personality quite likable. I haven’t given
her a clear answer yet, but once Caron’s situation is resolved, I intend to
properly address our relationship.

The final person was Noma. A palm-sized being—a spirit, invisible to ordinary
humans. We could see her because we could use \[Magic Vision\].

She prides herself as the first spirit chef, tirelessly researching magical

partnership.

Our first meeting wasn’t exactly pleasant, but now we get along well as
partners. We’ve even co-developed numerous magical tools.

So, what were the three of us doing? We were training Marina. Under Noma’s and
my guidance, Marina was repeatedly absorbing and releasing magical essence to
improve her magical skills.

However,

“My lord.”

“I know.”

I responded quietly to Noma’s somber tone.

Marina’s magical proficiency had likely reached its limit.

It’s not her fault. On the contrary, she’s worked incredibly hard.

The continuous cycle of \[Absorption\] and \[Release\] places immense strain on
the user’s mind and body. Thanks to Noma’s and my advice, she’s avoided

now, Marina was drenched in cold sweat as she persisted in her training.

How could someone who works so hard not grow stronger? The root cause was
simple.

—Growth limit.

With enough time, she might improve a little more. But Marina’s talent was
already tapped out. Her vessel had reached its peak, and dramatic improvement

Even so, this was a remarkable feat for the character of Marina. Originally just

standing among court wizards. If this doesn’t deserve praise, what does?

Let me say it again: Marina’s efforts are extraordinary. With ordinary
willpower, she wouldn’t have reached this level in less than a year—no, not even

But reality is always cruel. No matter how much effort she pours in or how

Still, Marina herself wouldn’t accept this. Driven by her desire to stand by my

“Maybe we have no choice but to find a spirit,” I muttered.

Marina’s last hope lay in becoming a spirit mage—the one talent she possessed

According to Noma, “A contract is possible when both parties feel a spark.” Our

Is this girl going to be okay?

I was quite worried, but this was a necessary step. Given her role, she’d be
spending more time with us in the future.

The role I envisioned for Skia was to assist or act as a substitute for Caron.

As her eyes indicated, she possessed an aptitude for light magic. Due to a

By the way, I’ve already confirmed that Skia can activate light magic.

The reason I chose today to introduce her to everyone was that her treatment for

Caron showed the most dramatic reaction to Skia’s arrival.

“Someone with the same light aptitude as me\! Let’s support Onii-sama

I had worried she might feel jealous, thinking her identity was being

There aren’t many student-involving incidents in the original story—just one

Yet, she strives to connect with her students, always prioritizing them. While

The world is full of people who think, “I suffered, so others should too.” Even

If she were always this charming, she’d be adorable, but in reality, she’s a

She must have been under a lot of stress. Despite her youthful appearance, she’s

stress.

“Sorry to jump right in, but I’ll keep my promise. During the upcoming field

Why was I here? I wanted to fulfill a certain promise before the field trip

“Onee-chan\!”

Of course, it wasn’t just me and the sisters. Leaving them alone would likely

Yudai, the hero, had restrained Rina in a chokehold while reasoning with her.

Will it snow tomorrow? That’s how reliable Yudai seemed.
