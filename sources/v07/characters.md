### **★ Protagonist**

**Name:** Zex Levito San Foranada  
**Epithet:** The White Ruler \-Albida Monarch- (Zex)

**\[Remarks\]**  
The protagonist of the novel. A reincarnated person.

In just six months, he has grown to the point where he can secure victories
against Akatsuki, a former Apostle of God. His ambition knows no bounds, and he
has now delved into abilities that transcend the human framework, such as \[Body
Enhancement: Divine Transformation\] and \[Time Stop\]. Perhaps the most
terrifying aspect is that he is training these powers in a way that 'does not
spread damage to the surroundings.'

He accurately understands his own abilities, being neither arrogant nor overly
humble. This is because he is aware that his talent does not exceed the bounds
of common sense. If one were to look at talent alone, his is likely lower than
any of the main members. The reason he has been able to reach the status of the
world's strongest is the result of undergoing rather unreasonable training. He
is a person who works too hard.

His greatest weapons are perhaps his observation skills and his ability to
devise countermeasures. It should be assumed that any tactic revealed to him
once will never work on him again.

The technology he is currently researching is the 'nullification of the Western
Demon King's nullification resistance.' It seems he is gradually making
progress.

He possesses two Magic Eyes.

His right eye is the **\[Blazing Flame Heaven Eye\]**. The eye changes to a
pattern of "a white iris with a red rim and several red lines," and a white
flame emerges. It is a fire-attribute Magic Eye. Its basic ability is to envelop
a target in white flames, which will burn endlessly down to the soul unless the
caster permits it to stop.

His left eye is the **\[Magic Eye of White Radiance\]**. The eye shines with a
divine, brilliant white light. It is a non-attribute Magic Eye. Its basic
ability is to expose everything about a target.

His magic has reached a realm that surpasses even that of the Magic
Administrators, without him becoming one. This is undoubtedly due to his efforts
and the life-and-death mock battles with the former Apostle of God.

He is a rare charmer recognized by everyone around him. While he naturally
possesses such qualities, the significant advantage of mental magic has had a
major influence. He is considerate, accurately understands what people desire,
is not indecisive, and possesses the overwhelming ability, status, and wealth to
solve problems. He is an incredibly dependable person with no reason not to be
fallen for.

His fiancées are a given. He also intends to accept the confessions of love from
Shion, Nina, and Marina in the near future. Regarding Caron, it seems he wants a
little more time to sort out his own feelings.

He possesses a strong sense of reason that can withstand even aggressive
approaches. It's not that his libido is weak; he simply suppresses it with
reason. While he may be a "Choroin" (easy to win over?), he is the type who
would never fall for a honey trap.

### **★ Heroines**

**Name:** Caroline Flamel ga Sali Foranada

**\[Remarks\]**  
The eldest daughter of the Foranada family. Zex's younger sister.  
She has a severe brother complex and became aware of her romantic feelings after
it was pointed out by a demon.

Having spent many years as siblings, she seems to understand that an immediate
confession is unlikely to succeed. Therefore, she is striving to make him see
her as a member of the opposite sex little by little and to secure her position
from the outside. However, all of this is completely transparent to Zex.

Bright, intelligent, and affable, she is treated as a madonna-like figure at the
academy. A so-called "flower on a high peak." Although no one approached her at
the beginning of the school year due to being intimidated by her brother Zex,
she now has a relatively wide circle of friends. However, after the individual
tournament, she is feared by some.

She belongs to the Magic Piece (Magi-Piece) club and, after a few twists and
turns, now serves as its president.

In combat, she specializes in high-firepower, wide-area magic. She has a
tendency to be a firepower idiot. She is also quite skilled in close-quarters
combat, using her fists or a giant hammer. Her favored weapon is the magic tool
"Charul," made by Zex. Like in the original story, it is beginning to evolve
into a sentient weapon.

As mentioned, her magical specialties are area-of-effect attacks and defensive
magic. She has trained particularly hard in the latter to ensure the regrets of
the past are never repeated.

Her jealous and persistent nature is unchanged from the Caroline of the original
work—it could be called an unchangeable trait. However, her desire for 'Brother
Zex to be happy' surpasses it. She has also grown to be genuinely fond of the
other women around him. This is why she was able to reject the temptation of the
demon.

It seems there is something special about her that has caught the attention of
the Western Demon King.

**Name:** Shion Schuhira Chromis

**\[Remarks\]**  
A servant of the Foranada household. Holds a secretarial position to Zex.

She loves Zex so much it's unbearable. When she is by his side, her cheeks relax
ever so slightly. This is a fact known by everyone in the Foranada household.
The only one who doesn't know is Shion herself.

In addition to being an illegitimate child, she left the dark side of the royal
court without a word, leading to a strained relationship with her family.
However, after the Sawaid family, her main family, made a major blunder and was
nearly wiped out, the overt conflict has disappeared. The two parties are now in
a state of near non-aggression.

She is extremely innocent. Even just holding hands makes her face turn bright
red. She is currently in the process of gradually getting used to it for the
future.

Although her appearances in the main story have been few recently, she spends by
far the most time with Zex, and they are actually quite lovey-dovey.

Also, her clumsiness is still present.

**Name:** Orca Falgata ga San Foranada

**\[Remarks\]**  
Zex's adoptive younger brother, who was adopted into the Foranada family.  
He has a cute appearance, a so-called "male daughter" (otokonoko). His looks are
so much like a girl's that everyone except the Foranada siblings often forgets
his gender. He wears a skirt for his uniform and often wears slacks and other
trousers for his casual clothes.

Due to his cheerful and friendly personality, he has many friends at the
academy. Because of his appearance, he has dragged many male students into the
swamp. Incidentally, he has received confessions (from both boys and girls)
several times but has not accepted any so far.

Although not to the extent of Caron, he has a more than sufficient brother
complex (limited to Zex). He is aware that his feelings for Zex are different
from those he has for his two biological older brothers, but he has yet to grasp
their true nature.

He has been involved in the management of the domain since before the Foranada
family stepped into the limelight. Combined with his natural intelligence, he is
now skilled enough to hold his own against veteran civil officials. He often
acts as Zex's substitute in emergencies.

In terms of intelligence, he probably ranks among the top in the Holy Kingdom.
He can be quite ruthless in strategic matters and is merciless towards his
enemies.

In combat, he does not possess overwhelming strength. However, his magic
manipulation skills are second only to Zex and Noma, and he can execute quite
dexterous techniques.

**Name:** Nina Gosilane Harneus

**\[Remarks\]**  
A former viscount's daughter, a former slave, and currently a rank A adventurer
with an epithet. She is also Zex's "fiancée-in-promise." Although "fiancée"
would be correct, she holds a peculiar attachment to the term
"fiancée-in-promise."

Due to her tall stature and cool personality, she has many female fans at the
academy, to the extent that an unofficial fan club has been established. She was
unaware of her popularity before coming to the royal capital, but she has
recently started to understand it.

Her pure combat power is top-class among the usual members. In an all-out
battle, she could probably achieve victory against anyone except Zex.

While her talent with the sword is exceptional, she is lacking in the magic
department. Well, even so, through special training, she has become able to
handle magic above an average level.

She has a twin sister and was the object of her obsession for a while recently,
but that has finally settled down. While they haven't completely cut ties, they
are maintaining a healthy distance from each other.

A fate of death had followed her since before she entered the academy. However,
she cut down the source of it with her own sword and broke free from her captive
destiny.

Her dream for the future is to be a bride, and she has made a promise with Zex.
Among the members who love him, she is the most aggressive with physical
affection. She hugs him at every opportunity and relentlessly pushes for kisses.

**Name:** Minerva Allreni yu Cali Loramber  
**Epithet:** The Color Commander \-Colors Commander-

**\[Remarks\]**  
The daughter of a ducal house and Zex's fiancée.  
She is a perfect woman who can do anything, but her cooking is actually
disastrous. Being a sore loser, she is training hard every day.

She has a so-called tsundere personality, but recently she has become able to
speak her true feelings.

No matter what difficulties stand in her way, her spirit will never break. She
possesses an indomitable fighting spirit. That is why, despite being a
latecomer, she has grown into a formidable person on par with Caron and the
others.

She is the most sensible person among the usual members. Often plays the
straight man (tsukkomi). A realist.

Perhaps due to the weakness of being in love, she is surprisingly sweet on Zex,
despite her complaints.

In combat, she is an all-rounder. Her lack of a standout specialty was a
concern, but with Zex's help, she is gradually beginning to show her true
potential.

Her personality compatibility with Zex is better than anyone else's. In the
original story, her capture difficulty was the highest, and there was even a
risk of divorce after clearing her route, but with Zex, there is absolutely no
need to worry.

Her recent concern is how to deal with Zex when he finally starts to turn his
attention to romance. Since her days are already filled with so much happiness
even when he isn't focused on it, she recognizes that the future will be even
more intense. Therefore, she is recommending increasing the number of concubines
and mistresses to reduce the amount of attention directed at her. However, Zex
is also doing his best not to decrease the time he can spend with each person
(such as by developing \[Time Stop\]), so Minerva's efforts are actually in
vain.

**Name:** Marina Aloela Cruz  
**Date of Birth:** February 1  
**Race:** Human  
**Blood Type:** O  
**Gender:** Female  
**Physique:** 158cm, 88/58/90 (F)  
**Appearance:** Dark blue hair, pale bluish-purple eyes, a single ponytail
draped over her right shoulder.  
**Aptitude:** Water (Strong) / Fire (Weak)

**\[Remarks\]**  
She was supposed to be a capture target heroine on the hero's side in the
original story—but now she is one of those who love Zex. It seems Zex's
intervention indirectly affected her.

She has a gentle personality and always wears a soft smile. Has a rather
laid-back disposition. She is not fond of conflict. Aside from her good looks,
she is an ordinary commoner woman you could find anywhere.

Due to a blunder by her childhood friend, the hero Yudai, she ended up in a
situation where her life was targeted by a noble. Zex extended a helping hand as
a way to save her. Since then, she has joined the Foranada members.

Although she is an ordinary country girl, she possesses communication skills
that would make even nobles' eyes widen. However, she is poor at "thinking from
a long-term perspective," which is essential for those in leadership positions,
so this can only be utilized as a skill for simply deepening friendships.
Nevertheless, it is an exceedingly outstanding ability.

...However, she becomes a klutz when she is in front of Zex. It's like a fan in
front of their favorite idol. This has been gradually improving recently.

Her talent for combat is almost non-existent. In addition, when she fainted from
magical power deficiency in the past, she absorbed Zex's magical power,
resulting in her gaining high-density magic. Normally, this would be an
advantage, but because her magic manipulation skills are rudimentary, it has
become a hindrance. Despite this, she continues to work hard solely out of a
desire to be by the side of the person she admires. As a result, where her
ability would normally be that of an ordinary person, she has developed skills
on par with a lower-class knight.

She was feeling anxious about her stagnating abilities, but in a dungeon, she
finally managed to form a contract with a spirit. Although the spirit had a
strong, quirky personality, she utilized her high communication skills and firm
beliefs to successfully form a bond.

She was in a different class at the time of enrollment but became Zex's
classmate from the third term of the first year.

In the future, she will likely settle down as Zex's beloved concubine.

As an aside, in the original game, she was the character with the largest bust.
—However, in reality, this has been surpassed by Caron's efforts. The power of a
bro-con is terrifying.

- Wears silver-rimmed glasses and a white coat.
- Long dark hair and pathologically pale skin.
- She is a beautiful woman.
- A witch of the Demon King cult who was operating secretly within the Holy
  Kingdom.
- She had her power boosted by being possessed by a demon.
- She managed to escape from Zex once, but he developed thorough
  countermeasures, and she was disposed of.

**〇 Mad Sueisa San Roeme**

- The head of the Count Roeme family. A mad researcher.
- He was researching the magic of magical beasts, but in the process, he
  tampered with a dungeon and caused a stampede.
- His crime was severe, and he was judged according to the laws of the Holy
  Kingdom.
- Since all his direct descendants were involved in the incident, a branch
  family member took over the Roeme house.
- Incidentally, no information was obtained about those who cooperated in the
  dungeon conquest.
