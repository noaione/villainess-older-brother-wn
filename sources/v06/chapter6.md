# Epilogue: Honestly

The assault on the royal capital, orchestrated by the Demon King Cult’s “Demon
Summoning Ritual,” inevitably caused injuries, but miraculously concluded with
zero fatalities. This was largely thanks to the swift actions of us, the
Foranada faction. If events had followed the original story, the toll of
casualties would have been far greater.

Incidentally, not a single member of the Foranada forces sustained so much as a
scratch. There isn’t a single weakling among my subordinates who’d be injured by
mere demons.

The fact that Foranada decimated the demons was witnessed by virtually every
citizen of the capital, effectively broadcasting our strength to all. According
to intelligence reports, some have even started calling us a “demonic realm”
behind closed doors. It’s a bit vexing, but since it’s drastically reduced the
number of fools who underestimate us, I’ll let it slide.

With so many citizens aware of our exploits, even the royal palace had no choice
but to acknowledge our achievements. Shortly after the incident, I was commended
and awarded the prestigious “Black Pearl Radiance Medal,” the highest honor for
defense.

During the award ceremony, I could sense the complex emotions of the palace
faction. They likely couldn’t stomach the rising prestige of their rival,
Foranada. My sympathies.

As for the academy, there were no significant changes before or after the
incident. Damage was kept to a minimum, so the second semester proceeded
smoothly, leading straight into winter break as December arrived.

Phew. My initial plan was a success. Rumors about me have been eradicated,
replaced by an air of reverence—or perhaps fear. Most people now glance at me
from a distance, their eyes tinged with apprehension. It’s a bit bothersome, but
it’s far better than being underestimated.

The winter break spans a full two months, a considerate arrangement allowing
students to return home for the new year. Unless you’re from an exceptionally
remote region, you can expect a relaxed holiday season.

We’re no exception. During the break, we’ll return to Foranada. Sure, with
the 【Phase Connection Gate】, we frequently visit home anyway, but this feels
like a proper homecoming. I’d love to stretch my wings and relax… if I can, that
is.

Tomorrow, everyone except the standby staff will return to Foranada Castle. The
annex was a bit hectic with preparations, but amidst the bustle, a visitor
arrived.

And that visitor was…

“Good day, Lady Skia. What brings you here today?”

Entering the reception room, I greeted the guest who had been shown in earlier.

Indeed, the visitor was Skia Thornbull Yu Ga Tali Cheyni, a viscount’s daughter.
A girl with peculiar tastes and a condition known as Phase Conflict Syndrome.

“G-G-G-Good day, E-Earl Foranada… Y-Your Excellency…”

As always, her interpersonal communication skills were lacking. Her eyes darted
nervously, her words stumbled, and her idle fingers fidgeted restlessly.

Her social awkwardness wasn’t new, so I let it pass with a wry smile, opting not
to comment.

However, her overly formal address needed correction.

“Please, no ‘Your Excellency.’ This is practically a private setting. Feel free
to call me by my name, as befits peers of the same year.”

“T-T-T-Tch, t-that’s… t-too great an h-honor…”

“At least drop the ‘Excellency.’”

“…U-Understood, Earl Foranada.”

I had no intention of forcing familiarity, so this was probably the limit. The
distance felt appropriate.

As the conversation paused, Skia hesitantly moved to the main topic.

“T-T-Tch, today, I c-came to… g-give my r-response to… y-your p-previous
p-proposal…”

As expected, that’s why she came. I’d asked out of formality, but there was no
other reason for her to visit me.

The proposal in question was this: in exchange for treating her Phase Conflict
Syndrome, I asked her to join Foranada. Initially, since I couldn’t provide
precise evidence, she requested six months to consider it, putting the matter on
hold.

It hasn’t even been three months, but it seems she’s reached a decision.

“Are you sure? You still have more than half the time left. You could take it
slow, maybe discuss it with your family during the winter break.”

Given the timing, she had ample opportunity to consult her family. Since this
concerns her future, I wanted her to think it over carefully.

Skia, however, shook her head vigorously.

“N-N-N-No… I-I’ve a-already… m-made my d-decision.”

“Hmm… If you’re confident in your choice, that’s fine. So, what’s your answer?”

It’s easy to forget while talking to her, but Skia is exceptionally intelligent.
If she says she’s decided, it’s likely a well-considered conclusion.

At my prompting, she took several deep breaths before speaking slowly.

“I-I… w-would like to… a-accept the t-treatment.”

“And you agree to join Foranada as well?”

“—Yes.”

Her nod was free of hesitation. Her golden eyes locked onto mine, conveying the
firmness of her resolve.

If she’s made up her mind, I have no objections. More hands on deck are a boon
for me, after all.

Receiving her answer, I nodded magnanimously.

“Very well. Let’s begin the treatment.”

With that, I promptly summoned my magical energy.

Skia, sensing the shift in my demeanor, widened her eyes.

“W-W-Wha\!? R-R-Right now\!?”

“Exactly. It’s not a complex procedure, so let’s get it over with.”

After all, we’re heading back to Foranada tomorrow. While we can return anytime,
I’d rather not bother opening the 【Phase Connection Gate 】 repeatedly. For
Skia, too, the sooner she’s treated, the better. Starting now benefits us both.

The spell for her treatment was developed long ago, with fine-tuning for Skia
already complete. All it needed was magical energy to activate.

Raising my right hand toward her, I focused and chanted the spell’s name.

“【Tuning】”

A mental magic spell that adjusts the target’s magical ratios to a desired
state. In this case, with Caron’s assistance in mind, I skewed it heavily toward
light attributes.

The magical energy released from my hand flowed into Skia, permeating her body
in an instant. In less than five seconds, 【Tuning】 was complete.

“It’s done.”

“Huh? A-Already?”

Skia muttered, stunned.

I nodded.

“For the first session, yes. The treatment will require multiple sessions, so
you’re not fully cured yet. Let’s do the next one… after the winter break. Doing
it too frequently could strain your body.”

Altering one’s innate magical ratios isn’t without risks. Since this is her
first treatment, we’ll monitor her progress carefully.

“…”

Skia remained dazed, staring at her hands with a puzzled tilt of her head. She
didn’t seem to feel the change.

With a sigh, I suggested, “Why not try casting a simple spell? Right after
treatment, you should be able to manage something.”

“Y-Yes.”

Snapping back to reality at my words, she raised her left hand and chanted,
“【Dark Sphere 】.” Choosing darkness over light showed her preference for
certainty—a clever choice, as expected.

The spell succeeded. A 【Dark Sphere】 floated above her palms. It was small and
crude, her first attempt at magic, but it was undeniably active.

“U-U-Uhh…”

Tears welled in Skia’s eyes at her first successful magic.

And why wouldn’t they? Despite her light affinity, she’d been unable to use
magic at all. The pressure she faced must have been immense. Casting a spell was
likely a lifelong dream for her.

After calming down, she returned home.

As she left, she declared, “I-I-I’ll n-never f-forget this k-kindness\!”

From her passionate words, I could tell Skia would work diligently for us.

It felt a bit like exploiting her weakness, but it’s a mutually beneficial
arrangement. Going forward, I look forward to working with her as a comrade.

---

In the Holy Kingdom, the new year is a lively affair. Except for young children,
everyone gathers to celebrate with enthusiasm.

Foranada is no different. Despite the late hour past midnight, the castle and
its surrounding town were brightly lit and bustling with noise.

I was currently on a balcony of Foranada Castle. I’d been mingling with my
subordinates at the New Year’s party but stepped out to cool off. The balcony
faced the town, so its lights and sounds reached me. It was reassuring to see my
people enjoying the moment to the fullest.

After basking in the cold night air for a while, the crisp winter midnight felt
refreshing, but a few minutes in, the chill began to bite. No matter how strong
I become, I can’t escape my human side.

Just as I turned to head back inside, I sensed someone approaching. The direct
trajectory and magical signature pointed to Minerva. She must have slipped away
from the party as well.

It felt callous to leave my fiancée waiting, so I decided to stay. To ward off
the cold without retreating, I enveloped myself in a thin layer of magical
energy.

“Ah, there you are.”

Minerva appeared, her first words spilling out softly.

I tilted my head. “Were you looking for me?”

“No, not quite. I came out here to cool off a bit. But I did notice you were
gone.”

“So it just slipped out?”

“Something like that.”

With a small smile, Minerva stepped beside me, resting her elbows on the balcony
railing.

She gazed quietly at the scenery before us. The sight of her exhaling white
breaths was utterly charming, quickening my heartbeat.

“Is something wrong?”

I must have stared too long, as Minerva tilted her head and asked.

I muttered, “Nothing,” and started to look away, but reconsidered. It was too
late for embarrassment. Returning my gaze, I smiled and spoke honestly.

“I was just admiring how adorable my fiancée is.”

“C-Cute\!? Admiring\!?”

My straightforward words hit Minerva’s weak spot. Her face flushed red in an
instant, and she stammered, mouth opening and closing. In a manga, steam would
surely be rising from her head.

Savoring her endearing reaction, I waited for her to recover.

Soon, she began deep breathing.

“You’re such a flirt…” she said, her cheeks still flushed but her ability to
speak restored.

I gave a wry smile. “A flirt? I was just being honest.”

“That’s what makes you a flirt\! How can you say such things without a hint of
embarrassment?”

“I’m plenty embarrassed. But we’re engaged, so I figured it’s better to praise
you openly.”

“Even engaged couples usually hesitate a bit\!”

Minerva shrugged with a sigh, as if exasperated.

Was I in the wrong here?

I wasn’t entirely convinced, but she didn’t seem upset. Her emotions radiated
joy and embarrassment—her usual way of hiding her feelings. She’s just too cute.

“Last year was quite eventful, wasn’t it?” Minerva said suddenly, shifting the
topic during a lull.

Curious about the abrupt change, I played along. “Yeah, a lot happened since we
enrolled at the academy.”

“I was shocked when you picked up a new girl right after enrolling.”

“That’s a harsh way to put it. I was protecting her.”

“But Marina fell for you.”

“Well, that’s…”

That stung. Marina developing feelings for me was entirely my oversight.

As I averted my eyes awkwardly, Minerva let out a cheerful laugh.

“Sorry, just teasing. I’m not mad.”

“It doesn’t feel like just teasing.”

“Hehe, sorry.”

Her laughter carried no malice as she apologized.

“Marina’s a commoner and a bit odd, but she’s a great girl. She’s smart and
courageous. Even if she ends up as a concubine, she’ll manage just fine.”

“It’s not decided yet, you know?”

“But you’ll probably accept her eventually, right? I can tell you’re drawn to
her.”

“Well…”

I was at a loss for words. It was true I was growing fond of Marina, and the
likelihood of accepting her was high.

Minerva laughed again, meeting my eyes directly.

“I don’t mind how many girls you take in. Sure, I’ll feel a bit jealous, but I
know you’ll shower us with love and warmth, even while focused on other goals.
You’ve already got me wrapped around your finger. That much I’m certain of.”

She added with a smirk, “If anything, you’d better take more, or I might burn
out.”

Well, she’s not entirely wrong.

Right now, I’m focused on averting Caron’s doomed fate, which leaves me little
time for Minerva and the others. I’d felt guilty about that, but according to
her, I’m already doing more than enough. If I save Caron, what awaits is likely
“love beyond measure.” I can already sense my restraint slipping.

Still, having my fiancée encourage me to take more lovers feels… questionable. I
wouldn’t mind a little jealousy, even if it’s selfish of me to think so.

I must have looked conflicted, as Minerva laughed again. She’s been laughing a
lot today.

“Hehe, I know you’re not actively seeking more girls. But mark my words,
there’ll be more. You’ve got the charm and capability for it. So, do your best.”

“Am I supposed to just nod and say ‘Got it’?”

“Exactly. Be confident. You’ve earned that right.”

“Got it. But—”

I paused, gently placing a hand on Minerva’s cheek and leaning in to kiss her.

“You’re my true wife, Minerva.”

It was a brief kiss, but I poured my affection into it.

It seemed to reach her. She turned away, her face red again.

“You’re so unfair… But don’t worry, I’ll do my best as your wife.”

No more words passed between us.

Yet, the air between us was warm—not physically, but emotionally.

I sincerely wished for our bond to remain like this forever.
