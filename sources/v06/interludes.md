# Interludes

## Shion Temptation (Part 1)

The timeline is around the "Invasion of the Devil" period.

In a house standing on the outskirts of the royal capital, I—Shion—along with
Lady Caroline, found ourselves in the basement.

On the surface, it appeared to be an ordinary building, but the basement was
anything but normal. The rough stone-walled room, measuring twenty meters on
each side, was completely covered in magical circles drawn in blood, emanating a
horrific stench of death. While there were no direct remains left behind, the
purpose of this place was easily discernible.

Now, this eerie room had become a ceremonial altar. In the center sat a
thirty-centimeter tall statue, radiating an overwhelming sense of dread.

“The suppression here is complete.”

“Perfect timing. I’ve finished here as well, Shion.”

Lady Caroline and I had been eliminating the members of the Demon king's Church
guarding this place, sharing the task between us. I thought we had dispatched
them fairly quickly, but it turned out we were done at the same time. I was
surprised that Lady Caroline managed to keep up, especially since indoor combat
wasn’t her strong suit... She was growing stronger by the day.

Looking over at Lady Caroline, I noticed she was the only one left standing. She
had likely obliterated the enemy without a trace. Since there was no need for
interrogation this time, it was fine to eliminate everyone, but her ability to
leave no remnants made me wonder if she was more suited to being an assassin
than I was. Yet, such bloody circumstances did not seem fitting for someone like
her.

For your information, although Lady Caroline was a noble lady from an earl’s
family, she had gone through the experience of taking lives. In fact, not just
her, but all the members of the Forlanada had undergone such experiences. This
was due to Zex-sama's policies.

The reasoning was that without having faced such experiences, one would struggle
to act when confronted with such situations. Unfortunately, in this world, human
lives were often treated lightly. Therefore, Zex-sama deemed it necessary for
Lady Caroline and the others to be prepared, even if he secretly wished they
wouldn’t have to face such a world.

However, there was no need for them to become accustomed to it. The important
factor was whether they had experienced it or not. The difference between having
or not having such experiences affected one's quickness to act in emergencies.
From what I could see, Lady Caroline seemed to be just fine. In fact, she was
remarkably efficient. Given her intense brother complex, she likely thought, “I
will eliminate every single enemy of my brother.”

Well, that was fine. Our priority now was to accomplish what Zex-sama had
ordered us to do. We needed to retrieve the statue placed in the center.

After confirming there were no ambushes, we approached the statue.

The statue was grotesque. It appeared to be modeled after a chalice, yet it was
formed from the bodies of the deceased. Piles of corpses shaped the chalice, as
if to say it had been constructed from the sacrifice of many lives. Moreover,
four demons sat equidistantly around the rim of the chalice. Unlike the numerous
demons currently infesting outside, these had a somewhat superior presence about
them. It was possible that if summoning continued, these demons would emerge as
well.

Nevertheless, there was no need to worry. Now that we were here—Zex-sama was
here—the Demon king's Church's plans would inevitably fail. I had no doubt about
that. Even if these four demons were to appear, I believed Zex-sama could crush
them with one hand.

“Is it safe to touch the statue directly?”

I asked Lady Caroline, who was a light mage, as an unfamiliar magical power was
flowing from the statue, prompting me to be cautious of any curses.

She nodded.

“There’s a strange magical energy flowing, but I don’t sense any curses or
similar threats.” Lady Caroline said.

“Then let’s retrieve it.”

I covered the statue with magic before lifting it.

As I did, the magical energy that had been flowing out suddenly dissipated. It
seemed this object ceased to function when it was not in its designated
position.

Afterward, I communicated with Zex-sama using mental transmission, and he helped
me retrieve the statue through phase linkage. He mentioned that only Lord
Ziglard could truly neutralize this statue. Hence, the plan was to gather all
the statues at his location.

As for me, I couldn’t help but think, Can’t Zex-sama erase that statue without a
trace? But I held my tongue regarding my master's policy. If he deemed it
necessary, it must serve some purpose.

Now that our mission was accomplished, our next step was to deal with the demons
rampaging outside and protect the citizens of the royal capital.

Caroline and I exchanged silent nods and returned to the surface.

However, we couldn’t participate in the demon extermination. An unwelcome guest
had appeared right before us.

—A demon.

The very name of the race surfaced in my mind. Dark skin, horns sprouting from
the temples, and an aura of thick curses emanating from him. Every
characteristic matched that of a demon. He was male, with somewhat long black
hair, but his physique was unmistakably that of a man.

Though I had received reports from Zex-sama, this was the real deal. Born from
the remnants of a sealed Demon king, this was a race plotting to resurrect him.
They were humanity's arch-enemy, appearing before generations of saints and
heroes.

Despite resembling a human in appearance, his strength was immediately apparent.
He could probably trample every knight in the Holy Kingdom by himself. This was
not an opponent to underestimate.

Yet, amidst my caution, I also felt relief. This demon was not our enemy. I was
certain he couldn’t harm us no matter what.

I lacked Zex-sama’s ability to accurately gauge others' strength, but I sensed a
clear disparity between us. Even if I were to overestimate, Caroline and I were
at least one and a half times stronger.

_Lady Caroline._

_Yes, I understand. As planned._

_Understood._

We exchanged brief messages through telepathy as we focused on the demon before
us.

In truth, I had anticipated this development. Zex-sama had foreseen that amid
the chaos of the demon attack, a demon would attempt to make contact with
Caroline.

In other words, our preparations were thorough. There was no chance we could be
defeated by this foe, and even if he attempted to flee, we were ready to pursue
him.

“Who are you?”

As a servant, I demanded to know.

“Oh, I have no use for you at all... But, let’s see, this might be more
entertaining,” the demon replied, wearing a creepy grin. He looked like a
mischievous child with a toy—or rather, a more sinister version. It was the face
of a criminal contemplating something malicious.

“Congratulations, you’ve been chosen. If you come with me, you’ll get everything
you desire: power, wealth, authority, love, you name it.”

The demon clapped his hands cheerfully, but we remained unmoved. We merely
continued to gaze at him.

Apparently, that didn’t amuse him. He paused his clapping and frowned slightly.

“You’re a tough crowd. Shouldn’t you have a bit more of a reaction? I expected
at least some joy or surprise.”

“Demons are enemies of humanity. It’s only natural to be cautious.”

“Ah, I see. You’re on guard. Ha ha ha, such a futile effort\! No matter what you
do, it won’t mean a thing to someone like you.”

As I responded, the demon nodded in understanding.

Futile effort, meaningless. His words indicated he was underestimating our
capabilities.

That was to be expected. After all, we were equipped with the magic tool
developed by Zex-sama known as Magic Power Concealment. We were deliberately
leading him to misjudge our strength, just as elves could perceive magic power
visually, demons can do the same.

The reason for this was simple: we could not allow the demon before us to
escape. Zex-sama had insisted that he needed to be dealt with at all costs.
Therefore, we were willing to overlook some danger in order to seize this
opportunity.

Unaware of our trap, the demon continued to speak carelessly.

“I won’t let you regret this. Deep down, you both harbor darkness in your
hearts. Right now, you have a chance to resolve that darkness.”

“Darkness in my heart?” I replied.

“Yes, the darkness in your heart. By manipulating that darkness just a little,
you can drastically enhance your power. If you gain strength, you’ll be able to
obtain whatever you desire.”

With that, the demon pointed at Caroline.

“Especially you. I sense a sweet, profound darkness coming from you. Is it...
jealousy? Such a rich and murky emotion. Hehe, it seems you love someone dearly.
That person is surrounded by much love, but you can’t stand that situation at
all.”

## Shion Temptation (Part 2)

“Especially you. I sense a sweet and profound darkness coming from you. Is it...
jealousy? Such a rich and murky emotion. Hehe, it seems you love someone dearly.
That person is surrounded by much love, but you can’t stand that situation at
all.”

Hearing this, I furrowed my brow.

I could see the truth in his words. He was undoubtedly referring to Zex-sama.

I suddenly recalled a conversation with Lady Caroline, I had right after
confessing my feelings to Zex-sama. During a private discussion, she had said,
“It’s not that I don’t feel jealousy.”

I was aware that Caroline had a jealous side, but I never imagined her feelings
were so dark and intense that even a demon would describe them as sweet. I had
assumed that despite her jealousy, she had come to accept the current situation.

It seemed that Lady Caroline felt similarly, as her expression had also clouded.

Ignoring our reactions, the demon continued to speak animatedly.

“Why not dispel that darkness? If you take my hand, you will definitely obtain
what you desire. You could monopolize the person you love.”

“Monopolize my brother...”

I inadvertently murmured the words. Lady Caroline looked stunned, deep in
thought.

This was truly a demon’s temptation. I hadn’t expected him to so precisely
strike at the weak points of Lady Caroline’s psyche. It seemed I had
underestimated the demon.

The demon wore a smug grin while Lady Caroline furrowed her brow in
contemplation.

As I observed their expressions, I pondered whether I should take action.

However, before I could make a move, the situation changed.

“Haah...”

It was Lady Caroline’s sigh that broke the painful silence.

She smiled wryly, lightly shrugging her shoulders.

“It seems that looking inward can be surprisingly difficult. I never thought I’d
come to realize it from a demon’s words.” Lady Caroline said in disappointment.

“People often don’t understand themselves the best. Now, take my hand.”

The demon eagerly extended his hand toward her.

However, Lady Caroline’s response was not what he had anticipated.

“What are you talking about, you lowly creature?”

Her voice was chillingly cold, a tone I had never heard from Caroline before.

Even I, not directly in her line of sight, felt momentarily frozen by the
intensity of her gaze.

Naturally, the demon, who was directly confronting her, stood completely still,
his expression one of confusion and shock.

“It’s impossible for me to take anyone’s hand but my brother’s, no matter how
the world turns upside down, even if I were to be reborn.”

Lady Caroline pressed one hand against her ample chest.

“As you mentioned, I love my brother. I do feel jealousy when that love is
directed elsewhere. Thanks to your comments, I’ve come to realize that those
feelings are greater than I had assumed.”

“B-but why won’t you take my hand\?! With my cooperation, you can obtain what
you desire\!”

The demon, breaking free from his shock, shouted with a high-pitched voice. His
desperate appearance made it clear just how mentally cornered he was.

The roles had completely reversed from just moments ago.

Hearing this, Lady Caroline let out a small laugh.

“That’s a separate issue. What do you think you can accomplish with the likes of
your power? In front of my brother, everything else is nothing but refuse. You
would just end up being defeated.”

Indeed, her words were accurate. Zex-sama’s strength was overwhelming. As it
stood, even if we attacked together, we stood no chance, so seeking help from a
small-time demon would be futile.

“And,” Caroline continued, “it seems I’m quite a greedy person. Despite feeling
jealousy, I also care for everyone. I cherish the time we all spend together
here. Therefore, I won’t take the hand of someone who claims to disrupt that.”

“Lady Caroline...”

To my surprise, I was moved by Caroline’s words.

I understood her feelings for Zex-sama, and now I recognized the depth of her
jealousy that had been hidden in the shadows.

I felt a swell of happiness in my heart, realizing that she directed such
affection toward us as well.

“That being said, I must ask you to leave. My brother has explicitly stated that
you must be eliminated.”

“Tch...”

With that merciless proclamation, Lady Caroline unleashed her magic. Eight
glowing pillars of light, each about five meters long, appeared and lunged
toward the demon, aiming to skewer him.

Of course, the demon wouldn’t go down without a fight. He attempted to evade and
counterattack simultaneously.

However, I needed to remember that Lady Caroline wasn’t the only one here. I
swiftly cast the tri-elemental composite magic, Tri-Bind, using dark, water, and
wind magic to impede the enemy’s movements.

Even for the demon, caught in the effects of three types of elemental magic, his
consciousness faltered. Lady Caroline wouldn’t miss this opening, and all the
light pillars pierced the demon, pinning him to the ground.

“Damn it, damn it, let me go\!”

Spitting blood, the demon desperately struggled to escape, but with a
significant difference in power, it was a futile effort.

“Why? Why am I caught by the likes of you?\!”

His speech deteriorated, likely a result of the pressure he felt from being
cornered.

Ah, that’s right. We had been concealing our magical power. From his
perspective, this situation must have been unbelievable.

Still, I had no intention of being overly honest about it. We maintained our
silence, finishing him off with Caroline’s light magic.

“Mission accomplished, then.”

Lady Caroline said with a refreshing smile, her demeanor seemingly lightened.

“Is something the matter?”

“No, it just gave me a chance to reflect on myself.”

“?”

I didn’t quite understand what she meant, but it seemed that this incident had
benefited Lady Caroline in some way. Since she didn’t appear to be hiding any
serious issues, I decided there was no need to press further.

After that, we participated in the hunt for the remaining demon remnants, and
the incident came to a close with a decisive blow from Zex-sama.

It had been a densely packed incident, but I was genuinely relieved that
everyone had managed to overcome it safely.

## Joobah, the Best Choice of the Century

The chronological order is from "Individual Battle" to "Demonic Attack".

---

My name is Joobah Stemhorus Kan Lorambel, the head of the duke’s family
governing the Lorambel territory.

Currently, I had traveled to the royal capital to observe the individual matches
of the first-year students in the academy. Ordinarily, I would watch the
third-year matches, but this time I was scheduled to see the first years
instead. More accurately, I would be observing the first years while leaving the
other grades to my subordinates.

Why was this year different? There were two reasons.

The first was that my daughter, Minerva, was participating.

For nobles, attending such school events for the children is not typically
something one would make time for. However, I had done the same for my two older
children in the past. While my affection for them was certainly a factor, the
primary reason was much more pragmatic. Since I would have opportunities to
utilize them in the management of my territory, it was essential to maintain a
good relationship. Therefore, I made a point of being involved in their
upbringing as much as possible.

For commoners, affection alone might suffice as a motivation, but for nobles, it
is not so simple. Unfortunately, one is required to make objective and rational
judgments about everything. This is even more true for a duke’s household.

The second reason was that I had heard Zex-sama, my daughter’s fiancé, would
also be participating. This was the main reason for my attendance.

He is extraordinary. He has established the previously dismissed attribute-less
magic as a practical form of magic, which many deemed meaningless. While it is
true that it is a type of magic that only he can wield, it is still astonishing
that he has realized it as a technique. Furthermore, he has even developed it
into imaginary arts such as storage magic and teleportation magic, which is
nothing short of astounding.

The knowledge I gained from him about Body Enhancement was groundbreaking.
Through precise visualization, one could not only eliminate the backlash from
Body Enhancement but also achieve further enhancement. This was an eye-opening
concept. To possess knowledge about bodily structure that is not widely
known—essentially monopolized by the church—and to apply that knowledge to magic
is something that left me in awe. No, it’s not just Body Enhancement; his
creativity is impressive. He breaks through all conventional wisdom and
continues to generate new forms of magic. He claims, “The people of this world
are bound by stereotypes. Magic should be much freer,” and I can’t help but
agree. Even I, known as a magic fanatic, tend to impose rigid definitions of
“what magic is.” His perspective is the truth.

With someone like him taking the stage in the individual matches, it was
impossible not to pay attention. I found myself eagerly awaiting this day,
feeling like a child again. My two sons were left astounded by my enthusiasm.

On the first day of the finals, after greeting Zex-sama and the others, I
arrived at the first-year match venue. I was waiting in the noble spectators'
seats for the event to begin, but I couldn’t shake off my nervousness.

Then, my second son, Hermes, who was allowed to accompany me, spoke up with an
exasperated tone.

“Father, please try to calm down a bit. No matter how anxious you are, the match
won’t start any sooner.”

“I understand that. But this excitement is something I simply can’t suppress.”

It was evident that my voice carried a hint of excitement. Ha, I shouldn’t be so
unrestrained as the head of a duke’s family.

“While I’m aware of your interests, aren’t you getting your hopes up too high?
From my perspective as someone who attended the academy until last year,
first-year matches are at a playtime level.”

“You’ve heard about Zex-sama from me, haven’t you? This year’s first-years are
distinctly different from previous years.”

“...Hah.”

As I laughed, Hermes let out a small sigh.

While his attitude toward me and the current head may not be commendable, I
suppose it’s acceptable. He tends to be a bit of a stickler. He must think that
I’m exaggerating my claims.

That said, I chose not to say much more. Once the match began, he would
understand whether he liked it or not.

Three days later.

I was deeply moved. Incredible\! Truly incredible\! I had expected Zex-sama's
magic to be extraordinary, but the skills of those gathered around him were
equally impressive. I never imagined that Minerva had also mastered
extraordinary techniques. At this rate, it was clear that she had already
surpassed my own abilities. Hehehe, before I return to my territory, I
absolutely must extract the details of those techniques\!

While I was overwhelmed with emotion, my son Hermes appeared dazed.

"My... common sense is... crumbling..."

He seemed to be clutching his head and muttering to himself.

Hmm. In the best light, he could be called pragmatic; in the worst, ordinary.
While he possessed four types of magical aptitude, his conventional thinking
apparently made Zex-sama's abilities feel like potent drugs to him. It seemed
best to leave him alone until he calmed down.

As I pondered this, the awards ceremony commenced. The faces of the winners from
other grades were in attendance, and for the most part, the results aligned with
our expectations. Major upsets are not something that occur often.

The ceremony proceeded smoothly, and just as it was about to conclude, the news
reached us abruptly.

"A mysterious monster has appeared throughout the royal capital and has begun
attacking the city\!"

"What\?!"

Receiving this alarming report, I questioned the messenger for more details.
What exactly is this monster? What is the enemy's strength? Which organization
is responsible? I gathered information from the messenger while dispatching
additional orders for intelligence gathering to various directions.

However, there was no time left to collect further information. The monster in
question had already surged into the venue where we were. Thanks to the barrier
that had been set up, we were safe inside, but the majority of people were
thrown into a panic. They began shoving and pushing each other in a desperate
attempt to escape.

I understood their feelings. The monster that had attacked was truly monstrous.
Its grotesque appearance could easily be described as demonic. With something
like that invading and starting to crack the barrier, it was unreasonable to
expect anyone to remain calm.

"Father, we should—"

—move. That’s what Hermes wanted to propose. However, he never had the chance to
finish his thought. Before he could, it was Zex-sama who acted.

_"Silence\!"_

His voice resonated in our hearts. The moment it reached my ears, my anxious
spirit flattened. It seemed I had been somewhat bewildered as well.

The reaction of the panicked crowd was dramatic. Like being doused with cold
water, they fell silent and focused their attention on Zex-sama.

Incredible\!

This was undoubtedly magic. I was convinced he possessed a form of magic that I
had yet to learn. Once this incident was resolved, I would have to ask him about
it right away.

While my spirits were lifted, they began to sink again.

Because—

"Father, am I seeing an illusion? The reinforcements from Foranada, who are
slaughtering the demons, appear to be nothing more than a mere maid."

"What a coincidence, I see it the same way. They all look like ordinary
servants."

To deal with the demons, Zex-sama had summoned reinforcements.

This in itself was fine, as I had known for some time that he could use
teleportation magic.

The issue lay with the composition of those reinforcements. As I mentioned
earlier, they all appeared to be servants. They were wearing butler and maid
outfits, the kind of plain servants you would find in any household. There were
a few knights among them, but the overwhelming majority were servants.

They faced the hordes of demons without hesitation, defeating them as if they
were the finest warriors. Those demons, which seemed to have the strength of
half a knight, were being dispatched one after another, unscathed, by what
appeared to be mere servants. It would not be an exaggeration to call them elite
fighters.

I had never wanted to praise myself more than I did today. By deciding to form a
connection with Zex-sama, I was certain I had made the best choice of the
century.

After the turmoil was subdued, it went without saying that I would take actions
to further strengthen our cooperative relationship with Foranada.

## Skia Decisive factor

The timeline is around "The Demonic Invasion".

---

Demons attacked the royal capital.

—Indeed. It was an ugly monster that was truly deserving of the title "demon."

They appeared suddenly. I was in the library, sorting through books and
preparing to take them back to my room. Just as I was about to catch sight of
the dormitory, I—Sukia—was attacked by a demon.

There was just one of them. From what I later heard, its individual strength
wasn't that remarkable. However, it posed a significant threat to me.

Because I had no ability to fight back. I couldn't use magic at all, and I
lacked any talent in martial arts, leaving me completely defenseless against
external threats.

So I ran. The moment the demon came into view, I turned on my heel and fled
without a second thought. There was no time to hesitate. I instinctively
understood that any delay could cost me my life, so I ran as if my life depended
on it.

What a dreadful day this was. Now that I think about it, it seemed like I had
been cursed from the morning. For breakfast, I had to eat those dreadful cherry
tomatoes I hated, and just before heading out, my shoelaces broke. At the
library, my favorite bookmark mysteriously got torn, and the book I had my eye
on was checked out (and overdue). And now I was being chased by a demon. I
wanted to believe I was cursed.

Had I done something wrong?

I hesitate to say it myself, but I take pride in having lived a well-behaved
life. Despite having the aptitude for light magic, I couldn't use it and ended
up causing my family a lot of trouble, yet they continued to treat me kindly
without change. I had tried to repay them by leading a peaceful life.
Admittedly, I can't boast about my taste in books, but I believed there were no
other issues.

Was it possible that God bore a grudge against me? Why was I being subjected to
trial after trial when I was so powerless?

Tears brimmed in my eyes at the thought of a life full of downhill struggles.
Besides that, I had been running for quite some time, and my heart hurt while I
struggled to breathe. For someone like me, who was more of an indoors person,
running long distances was nothing short of reckless. If I had known this would
happen, I regretted not exercising a bit more regularly.

How was it that, despite my awkward running style, the demon hadn’t caught up to
me yet?

As that fleeting thought crossed my mind, I glanced back over my shoulder.

The demon was still there. With its large mouth gaping open, revealing sharp
fangs, claws that could easily slice my neck, bulging eyes, and wings that
looked like they belonged to a mass of flesh. Just looking at it filled me with
disgust as it followed closely behind me.

Observing the demon’s behavior, I realized something. It was toying with me. It
found pleasure in watching me run away in terror, which was why it was taking
its time to chase me. It wanted to keep watching my pathetic escape and was
holding back from attacking.

What had I done to deserve this\?! I just wanted to live a normal life, so why
was I subjected to this?

My feelings of self-pity and helplessness surged up from the depths of my being.
My heart was a chaotic mix of anger and sadness.

My life was always like this. I couldn’t use magic, couldn’t fight back against
the whispers of those around me, and ultimately ended up burdening my family. I
had left the cleanup to them. They all smiled and forgave me, but I couldn't
forgive myself. That was why I decided to live a safe life. While great success
was beyond me, I made an effort to avoid becoming a further burden to my family.

Yet, despite all that—

"Ah."

It seemed my legs had reached their limit. Overcome by exhaustion, I could no
longer move properly and fell spectacularly to the ground.

There was no way someone as unathletic as I was could brace for impact, and I
crashed painfully onto the ground. I managed to extend my arms forward, but I
scraped them badly. It hurt.

The demon let out a displeased laugh at my miserable state and approached me
closely. It kept laughing loudly, observing me for a moment before leaning in
with its gaping mouth. It intended to eat me.

"Mom, Dad, everyone—I'm so sorry."

The faces of my parents and siblings floated into my mind. I hoped they could
forgive me for not being able to repay them properly.

I closed my eyes and awaited the final moment.

However, the pain I expected never came.

Given the nature of that vile demon, I thought it would take its time and eat me
starting from my extremities, but had it actually killed me in one swift bite?
...No, that wasn’t it. I could still feel the pain from my scraped hands and
feet. I was alive.

Cautiously, I lifted my closed eyelids.

What I saw before me was astonishing. The demon that had been right in front of
me was impaled by countless earth spikes that jutted up from the ground.

It was probably a low-level earth magic spell called Earth Spike. Someone had
defeated the demon that was about to attack me.

"I-I was saved..."

It was a moment where I had narrowly escaped death. Perhaps due to the relief, I
suddenly felt an overwhelming wave of fatigue wash over me. I became acutely
aware of my pounding heart and the copious amounts of sweat covering my body. It
was as if my whole being was experiencing pain, serving as proof that I was
alive.

As I desperately tried to catch my breath, I heard a woman's voice from a
distance.

"Hey, are you okay?"

"Garna, we’re in front of others, you know."

"Eh—it's an emergency, so isn’t it fine?"

"Garna?"

"Yes, yes, I understand. I'll adjust my tone. Is this good enough?"

"...Let’s consider it acceptable."

"Fwaaaaaaah. Just barely made it—should be right on time."

I couldn’t see them clearly because I was out of breath, but it seemed to be
three women. Their conversation lacked any tension, and one of them was yawning
dramatically. The contrast with the life-and-death situation was striking.

The three of them had arrived near me and, after observing my condition, began
to speak.

"It looks like you have no visible injuries."

"But you're really out of breath. It seems like it was a very close call."

"This girl is the daughter of the Cheeni viscount’s branch family—she might have
made quite the achievement?"

"Oh, that's true. I’ve seen her name in the documents."

"I see. I had heard that she couldn’t use magic, so she must have been in a real
pinch."

From the context, it seemed they were servants from some noble family. The fact
that they were familiar with my documents suggested they were not from the
common class.

Ninety-nine out of a hundred, they served the Foranada family. No other family
would refer to my rescue as a "great achievement." Right now, I was a failure
who couldn’t use magic at all.

Before long, I managed to calm myself. Though my breathing was still rough, I
was finally able to act normally.

I lifted my gaze to look at the faces of my saviors.

As expected, they were servants of Foranada. I recognized their faces; they were
indeed attendants to Count Foranada and his siblings.

"Th-thank you so much for saving me from such a dangerous situation\!"

Ah, why couldn’t I express my gratitude properly? Ever since I was young, I’ve
struggled to talk to others. I genuinely wanted to convey my thanks, but for
some reason, my mouth wouldn’t cooperate. It was frustrating and pitiful.

But there was no time to wallow in self-pity. After all, these were my
lifesavers, and I needed to show my sincerity.

"I-I will definitely repay you for this..."

As I stumbled over my words, I managed to finish my thanks, and the three of
them exchanged puzzled glances.

Their unexpected reaction left me similarly dumbfounded.

Then, one of them waved her hand lightly and replied, "You really don’t need to
worry about it that much. We’re just following the orders of our master to

## Louise Dazzling

The atmosphere in the room was thick with tension.

"The Second Prince, then..."

"Yes. That incident has effectively closed off any chance of Foranada opening

confrontation."

Even prioritizing emotions, I thought that Count Foranada was capable of

Princess Aria, a proponent of rationalism, prioritized risk management—not her

"There’s no need to be so serious. I wish to ‘do something’ about Foranada, not
eliminate him. If I can put a collar on him, there’s no need to resort to
hostility. That would be irrational."

"...As you wish, Princess."

As her sword and shield, I could not strongly oppose her declaration to pursue

However, if a fatal step were to be taken, I would—

In hindsight, Princess Aria might have been captivated by that extraordinary

vision. No matter how brilliant a person may be, one cannot discern the path
forward with clouded eyes.

I would come to regret my responses during this time. The magic had already

“The finals were insane\!”

Zara-san, having recovered from her laughter, nodded with sparkling eyes.

“It truly felt like a magical battle. Everyone already knew that the daughter of
Duke Rorambel was a skilled mage, but the real surprise was the Count of
Foranada. I had never seen magic like that before\!”

“Right? Right? My brother is amazing\!”

With the topic of my brother coming up, I couldn’t stay downcast any longer. I
nodded deeply in agreement with the discerning Zara-san.

At that moment, Ivette-san added to the conversation.

“Since I had heard about Caron-san from before, I knew that the rumors were
exaggerated, but that was beyond my expectations. Now I can wholeheartedly
accept that the rumor about the Count of Foranada defeating the Sword Saint was
true.”

“That’s right\! After all, my brother is the strongest in the world\!”

“The strongest... With that kind of performance, it’s certainly a plausible
claim.”

Not “plausible,” but a fact; he truly is the strongest... Well, I can understand
why it might be hard to believe, so I won’t press the issue. Besides, it seems
my brother wasn’t even serious when he fought Minerva, so I can’t blame them.

Suddenly, I remembered something.

“By the way, you two have never had the chance to greet my brother, have you?
Shall I introduce you next time?”

For some reason, Zara-san and Ivette-san hadn’t run into my brother.

They exchanged glances and let out a wry smile.

Then Zara-san made a shocking statement.

“Um... Actually, we’ve already met him.”

I widened my eyes in surprise.

“Wait, when did that happen?\!”

“When we were separated from you, we were guided by that maid over there.”

“Shion\?!”

When I turned to look at Shion standing behind me, she merely bowed with a
composed expression. The fact that she didn’t deny it confirmed that it was
indeed true.

At that moment, Ivette-san added an explanation.

“The Count of Foranada is the current head of his house, so he’s not someone a
mere noble girl can meet casually, even if they’re both students.”

“Well, that’s...”

I recalled that my brother had made a similar statement when discussing friends
with Minerva before.

“However, I think he arranged for the introduction because he was worried about
Caron-san.”

With that, Ivette-san concluded.

It hit me again: I—we—are always protected by the adults around us, led by my
brother. While Ivette-san and Zara-san are wonderful friends, if there were any
ulterior motives, they might have handled things secretly.

—I want to become an adult soon.

If that happens, perhaps we could be of help to my brother as well.

I couldn’t help but wish strongly and fervently for that.

That said, impatience is not advisable. If I rush things and make my brother
worry, it would defeat the purpose entirely.

So for now,

“I think it’s time to interrogate you both about why you kept this meeting a
secret from me.”

“Huh\?!”

Zara-san and Ivette-san gaped in surprise.

What are they so shocked about? The secret meeting was arranged on my brother's
side, so there was no need for them to hide that fact from me.

Moreover, their current reaction... It’s suspicious. I’ll make sure they
confess\!

That day, it was said that the screams of two female students echoed throughout
the academy, or perhaps they didn’t.
